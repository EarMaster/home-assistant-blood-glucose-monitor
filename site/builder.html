Automation builder

Pick the events and the phone; the builder prepares one automation that sends a notification with the ready-made text of the integration. Adjust it in Home Assistant if you want more.

Secondary notifications only. These notifications can be delayed or missing. Keep the alarms of your CGM, insulin pump or medical app switched on.

Prefilled from Home Assistant: only the events that are set up for this person are shown, with their entity IDs.

Tip: open the builder from Home Assistant (device page of the person → Visit, or Configure of the entry) to get the right entity IDs. Here they are guessed from the name; check them under Settings → Devices & services → Entities.

The notify action of the phone, for example notify.mobile_app_pixel_8 (see Developer tools → Actions). Several, separated by commas.

Events

Started or repeated → notification. Acknowledged or over → the notification is removed again.

Automation

In Home Assistant: Settings → Automations & scenes → Create automation → Create new automation, then ⋮ → Edit in YAML, paste and save.

When it fires

Select at least one event.
