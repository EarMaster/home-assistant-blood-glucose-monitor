Blood Glucose Monitor for Home Assistant

A custom integration that turns the glucose data of another integration (for example Nightscout or Dexcom) into optional hints for everyday life: alarms, reminders and reports, one entry per person. The integration decides when; you decide how you are notified.

Not a medical device. Not a replacement for real alarms. Blood Glucose Monitor only adds extra hints to Home Assistant. It does not replace the alarms of your CGM, insulin pump or medical app (for example Dexcom, LibreLink, CamAPS FX or xDrip+). Keep those alarms switched on at all times. Notifications from Home Assistant can be delayed or not arrive at all, for example when Home Assistant, your network or the glucose data source is down. Never rely on them alone. Use at your own risk; there is no warranty of any kind.

What it does

Glucose alarms

Urgent low, low, high, urgent high, rapid fall, rapid rise and data loss, each with delay, repeat, acknowledge, snooze and re-check. Urgent low is never paused.

Reminders

Charging reminder for the phone in the evening, and change reminders for cannula, CGM sensor and insulin reservoir with an optional warning before they are due.

Reports

A summary per period of a schedule (for example night or school): minimum, maximum, mean, time in range and the alarms of the period.

Your notifications

Every alarm and the report has an event entity. Your own automations decide how to notify: phone, speaker, light. The automation builder prepares them.

Secondary by design

The integration is meant as a second line: extra information for the household, a hint on the speaker at night, a reminder that the phone needs charging. It depends on Home Assistant, the network, the glucose integration and the phone, and any of these can fail without warning. The primary alarms belong to the CGM, the pump or the medical app.

  • Without a reading time of the glucose data there are no glucose alarms; a repair issue tells you so. Nothing is guessed.
  • Stale data neither starts nor ends a glucose alarm; the data loss alarm covers missing data.
  • Alarm states survive a restart of Home Assistant.

Getting started

  1. Install Blood Glucose Monitor from the Marketplace: add EarMaster/home-assistant-blood-glucose-monitor as a custom repository of type Integration, download it and restart Home Assistant.
  2. Add the integration under Settings → Devices & services, once per person: confirm the notice, enter the name and select the glucose sensor and, optionally, the person.
  3. Under Configure, switch on what you need: thresholds for the alarms, the charging and change reminders, the report periods.
  4. Create the notifications: open the automation builder from the device page of the person (Visit) or from the Configure menu. It already knows the entities of that person.

Entities per person

EntityPurpose
Status sensorThe most important current state, with the latest glucose value, trend and active alarms as attributes.
… alarm / … reminder eventTrigger for notifications. One per alarm; fires on start, repeat, acknowledge and end.
… alarm / … reminder sensorState of the alarm (idle, pending, active, snoozed) for dashboards and conditions. It does not change on a repeat.
Acknowledge … buttonAcknowledges the alarm for its snooze time.
Pause alarms switchSilences all alarms except urgent low for the configured time.
… change due sensor, … changed buttonDue time of a change reminder and recording a change.
Report event, Last report sensorThe report of each period; the sensor holds the time in range of the last one.
